Two sides, one app

The same app, facing both ways.

One account can be a worker, a venue, or both — the whole app flips to match.

Set your roles. Get the right jobs.

Tell Cloche what you do — bartender, chef, barista, whatever — and the suburb you're in. Your feed ranks nearby jobs by the roles you picked, with a plain-English summary above every ad.

  • One-tap applications with your certs attached
  • A map of every open job around you
  • Post your availability and let venues come to you

Post a shift. Meet the shortlist.

Put up a casual shift or a permanent role in a minute. Applicants arrive with verified credentials, distance and experience already on the card — and your feed shows workers posting their availability nearby.

  • Verified RSA, RCG and Food Safety before you message
  • Distance and experience on every applicant card
  • Message workers directly, no middleman

What it does

Built around four ideas.

A feed that knows your trade

The ranking is structural: roles you selected always outrank roles you didn't, and nothing — not freshness, not popularity — can push a wrong-role job above a right-role one.

All the personalisation happens on your phone. Your behaviour never leaves it.

See who's hiring near you

A live map of open jobs, with suburb pins sized and coloured by how many are there. Tap a pin, see the jobs.

Right now that's 314 jobs across 48 Sydney suburbs.

Credentials, verified once

Upload your RSA, RCG and Food Safety certificates once. Cloche verifies them and tracks expiry.

Venues see the green badge before they message you — no more emailing photos of cards.

Applicants, already filtered

Venues get a shortlist, not a pile. Every applicant card carries verified credentials, distance and experience up front.

When you hire someone, the role and the conversation stay together in one place.

Pull to refresh.
The cloche lifts.

The app is named after the serving dome. Pull the feed down and it lifts off the plate. That's it. That's the feature.

The rest of it

Small app, sharp edges.

AI summaries

Every listing gets a one-line plain-English summary above the raw ad. A wall of ALL-CAPS text becomes scannable.

Ranked feed

Your roles come first, structurally. The server sends candidates; your phone does the ranking, in private.

Suburb map

Pins sized and coloured by open jobs, filterable by role. See demand at a glance before you move.

Verified certs

RSA, RCG and Food Safety, uploaded once, verified and expiry-tracked. Venues see the badge, not a photo of a card.

Direct messaging

Workers and venues talk to each other, not to an agency. Applications, hires and chats stay in one thread.

Worker and venue in one

The same account can hold both sides. Flip it and the feed shows jobs to workers, workers to venues.
